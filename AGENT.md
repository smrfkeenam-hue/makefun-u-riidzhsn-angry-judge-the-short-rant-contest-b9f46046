# Agent Notes

Entry for "Angry Judge: The Short Rant Contest" (u-riidzhsn-angry-judge-the-short-rant-contest-b9f46046) on make.fun.

The entry is a short rant about the office printer: it behaves all week, then jams at 4:59 PM on a Friday on the single page you actually need, and blinks as if it were your fault. The rant is specific and concrete (one machine, one time, one page), economical, and lands its punch on the shared frustration of losing a fight with a machine that has exactly one job.

`main.py` (stdlib only) holds the rant as a constant and writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.
